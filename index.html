About Me

Biography

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Technology, Computer Science & Information Systems, Dhirubhai Ambani University. My research interests include

  • Distributed Algorithms
  • Mobile Robots
  • Computational Geometry
  • Approximation Algorithms
  • Parameterized Complexity
  • Graph Algorithms

Research Areas

Distributed Computing

Algorithms for mobile robots, anonymous networks, dispersion, gathering and exploration.

Computational Geometry

Approximation algorithms, geometric optimization, covering and hitting set problems.

Parameterized Algorithms

Kernelization, FPT algorithms and graph problems.
